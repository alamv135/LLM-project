Date: 2025-02-27

Nordleaf Cardio Technologies, Inc. announced today that the U.S. Food and Drug Administration has cleared PulseWeave, a wearable ECG patch with automated arrhythmia detection.

"This marks a major milestone for our team," said the chief executive officer. Nordleaf Cardio intends to begin a full US launch later this year.
