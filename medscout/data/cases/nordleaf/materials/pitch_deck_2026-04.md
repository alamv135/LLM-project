## Slide 2 — The problem
Atrial fibrillation often goes undetected until a stroke occurs. Short Holter recordings miss intermittent episodes.

## Slide 3 — Our product
PulseWeave is a flexible, single-use ECG patch worn on the chest for up to 14 days. Its embedded algorithm flags suspected arrhythmias for physician review.

## Slide 4 — Regulatory
PulseWeave is FDA approved for the detection of atrial fibrillation in adults.
A 510(k) submission for PulseWeave Pro, our 30-day next-generation patch, is planned for Q3 2026.

## Slide 5 — Clinical evidence
Our pivotal multicenter PULSE-AF study completed enrollment of 1,200 patients in 2025.
PulseWeave detects atrial fibrillation with 98% sensitivity.
Our validation results were published in a peer-reviewed cardiology journal.

## Slide 6 — Market and competition
Our main competitors are Corvane Diagnostics and Ashby Monitoring.
Unlike legacy monitors, PulseWeave needs no wires or recorder box.
