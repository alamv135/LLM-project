# US FDA device pathways — quick reference (for wording checks)

| Pathway | Typical device class | Outcome wording | Public record (openFDA) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 510(k) premarket notification | Mostly Class II | "cleared" — found substantially equivalent to a legally marketed predicate device | device/510k (K-number) |
| De Novo classification request | Novel low/moderate risk, no predicate | "granted" — creates a new classification | De Novo database (DEN number) |
| Premarket Approval (PMA) | Class III | "approved" — based on evidence of safety and effectiveness | device/pma (P-number) |

Notes
- "FDA approved" for a 510(k)-cleared device is a common but inaccurate description.
- Breakthrough Device designation, registration & listing, or an accepted submission are NOT marketing authorisation.
- A clearance applies to the specific device and indications described in the decision; later versions or
  new indications may need a new submission.
- FDA authorisation does not establish insurance coverage, coding or payment (CMS processes are separate).
- Devices sharing a three-letter product code are the same generic device type: a useful starting point for
  comparable products, not evidence of market share.
- openFDA is a public dataset with update lags; absence of a record is evidence of absence only after
  name variants were tried.
